River Rush Adventure

River Rush Adventure is an exciting intermediate rafting trip through fast-moving rapids and beautiful river scenery. The trip lasts about 4 hours and is suitable for adventurers ages 12 and older.

  • Difficulty: Intermediate
  • Duration: 4 hours
  • Age: 12+
  • Price: $75 per person

Canyon Explorer

Canyon Explorer is a beginner-friendly rafting trip through a beautiful scenic canyon with gentle rapids. The three-hour adventure is a great choice for families and new rafters ages 8 and older.

  • Difficulty: Beginner
  • Duration: 3 hours
  • Age: 8+
  • Price: $55 per person

Whitewater Challenge

Whitewater Challenge is an advanced rafting adventure designed for experienced rafters who enjoy powerful rapids and exciting challenges. This five-hour trip is available to adventurers ages 16 and older.

  • Difficulty: Advanced
  • Duration: 5 hours
  • Age: 16+
  • Price: $100 per person
